Nvidia's board accepted Jensen Huang's resignation on Nov. 11, 2026, less than two weeks after The New York Times reported that U.S. intelligence officials believe a former executive of Nvidia customer Megaspeed is a Chinese intelligence asset. This documentary explainer separates what is confirmed in filings and statements from what is only reported or alleged, and covers what to watch next: Nvidia's Nov. 17 earnings, the board's investigation and Huang's testimony to Congress.

Allegations are unproven; Nvidia, Megaspeed and China deny wrongdoing.

As of Nov. 14, 2026. Nvidia's board said it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised. Huang said he would cooperate fully with the investigation. China's foreign ministry called the report "unsubstantiated." Megaspeed denies wrongdoing and says it takes export rules "extremely seriously."
