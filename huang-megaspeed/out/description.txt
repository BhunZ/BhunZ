Nvidia's board accepted Jensen Huang's resignation on Nov. 11, 2026, two weeks after The New York Times reported that U.S. intelligence officials believe a former executive of Nvidia customer Megaspeed is a Chinese intelligence asset. This explainer separates what is confirmed from what is only reported or alleged, and covers what to watch next: Nvidia's Nov. 17 earnings and Huang's expected testimony to Congress.

Allegations are unproven; Nvidia, Megaspeed and China deny wrongdoing.

As of Nov. 14, 2026. Nvidia's board said it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised. Huang said he would cooperate with the board's review. China's foreign ministry called the claims groundless. Megaspeed denies wrongdoing and says it complies with export-control rules.
